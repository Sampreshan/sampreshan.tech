Whenever any project needs the ShivBodh Trust stamp / seal / mohar (certificates,
e-certificates, receipts, letters, PDFs, specimens), use **these files only**.
Do not redraw, recolour, re-letter or generate a new seal, and do not use the older
`programme-seal.svg` or canvas-drawn seals any more.

## How to place it

- Place on top of the paper with `mix-blend-mode: multiply`, a small rotation
  (−8° to −14°) and opacity 0.88–0.95 so the paper shows through like real ink.
- Overlap the signature/"For ShivBodh Trust" line slightly, as a real stamp does.
- Show the full stamp (do not crop away the registration details).
- Minimum printed size ≈ 35 mm; on screen keep it ≥ 110 px so the text stays readable.
- On canvas (PNG downloads), draw the `.png` with
  `globalCompositeOperation = 'multiply'`.
- Copy the needed files into the project's own repo (project isolation); do not
  hot-link this folder or another site's copy.
