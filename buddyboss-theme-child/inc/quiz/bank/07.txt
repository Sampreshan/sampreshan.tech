title: Adbhut Sanatan
The sanskara performed at the birth of a child is called what? | Jatakarma | Namakarana | Annaprashana | Vidyarambha
The sanskara of a child's first outing to see the Sun is called what? | Nishkramana | Karnavedha | Mundan | Upanayana
The ceremony for an expectant mother in the seventh or eighth month is called what? | Simantonnayana | Namakarana | Vivaha | Samavartana
The chart drawn at the time of a child's birth is called what? | Janma Kundali | Panchang | Muhurta | Yantra
The yearly rite for ancestors in the dark fortnight of Ashvin is called what? | Pitru Paksha | Navaratri | Kharmas | Adhik Maas
Offering rice balls (pinda) to the ancestors is part of which rite? | Shraddha | Vivaha | Upanayana | Griha Pravesh
Which city is especially sacred for performing Pinda Daan for ancestors? | Gaya | Pushkar | Nashik | Shirdi
According to the Bhagavad Gita, the soul discards old bodies as a person discards what? | Worn-out clothes | Old houses | Dry leaves | Broken pots
Which city is believed to grant moksha to those who die there? | Kashi (Varanasi) | Mumbai | Delhi | Pune
The sacred fire around which the couple walks during a Hindu wedding is witnessed by which deva? | Agni Deva | Indra Deva | Varuna Deva | Vayu Deva
The seven steps taken together by the bride and groom are called what? | Saptapadi | Kanyadaan | Vidaai | Haldi
The giving away of the bride by her parents is called what? | Kanyadaan | Saptapadi | Vidaai | Tilak
Mahashivaratri is dedicated to which deity? | Lord Shiva | Lord Vishnu | Lord Ganesha | Surya Deva
How many forms of Hindu marriage are described in the Manusmriti? | Eight | Four | Six | Ten
Giving without expectation of return is praised as which kind of dana in the Gita? | Sattvic dana | Rajasic dana | Tamasic dana | Kamya dana
The donation given to a priest or teacher after a ritual is called what? | Dakshina | Prasad | Bhiksha | Tarpana
Giving food to the hungry is called what? | Anna Daan | Go Daan | Vidya Daan | Bhoomi Daan
Which king is famous for giving away his kingdom to keep his word to Vishwamitra? | King Harishchandra | King Janaka | King Bharata | King Nala
Which warrior of the Mahabharata is famed as the greatest giver (Danveer)? | Karna | Arjuna | Bhima | Duryodhana
Which fast is observed on the eleventh day of each lunar fortnight? | Ekadashi | Purnima | Amavasya | Pradosh
The fast observed by married women for their husband's long life in Kartika is called what? | Karva Chauth | Chhath | Janmashtami | Ram Navami
The fast observed on the thirteenth lunar day in honour of Shiva is called what? | Pradosh Vrat | Ekadashi | Sankashti | Purnima
The fast in honour of Lord Ganesha on the fourth day after the full moon is called what? | Sankashti Chaturthi | Ekadashi | Pradosh | Navami
Chhath Puja is devoted to which deity? | Surya Deva and Chhathi Maiya | Lord Shiva | Lord Ganesha | Goddess Saraswati
How many varnas are described in the classical texts? | Four | Two | Three | Six
In the Bhagavad Gita, Krishna says the four varnas were created based on what? | Guna and karma | Birth only | Wealth | Region
How many ashramas, or stages of life, are there? | Four | Two | Three | Five
Which ashrama is the householder stage of life? | Grihastha | Brahmacharya | Vanaprastha | Sannyasa
Which ashrama is the stage of complete renunciation? | Sannyasa | Vanaprastha | Grihastha | Brahmacharya
Which ashrama is the stage of student life? | Brahmacharya | Grihastha | Vanaprastha | Sannyasa
Touching the feet of elders to seek blessings is called what? | Charan sparsh | Pradakshina | Abhisheka | Aarti
The ceremony of entering a new house is called what? | Griha Pravesh | Bhoomi Pujan | Namakarana | Shilanyas
The ceremony before laying the foundation of a building is called what? | Bhoomi Pujan | Griha Pravesh | Kanyadaan | Namakarana
Why is the greeting "Namaste" given? | To honour the divine in the other person | To ask for food | To end a conversation | To show anger
The cycle of birth, death and rebirth is called what? | Samsara | Moksha | Svarga | Dharma
The law of cause and effect of one's actions is called what? | Karma | Maya | Lila | Dharma
Accumulated karma from all past lives is called what? | Sanchita Karma | Prarabdha Karma | Kriyamana Karma | Agami Karma
The part of past karma that is shaping the present life is called what? | Prarabdha Karma | Sanchita Karma | Kriyamana Karma | Nishkama Karma
Freedom from the cycle of rebirth is called what? | Moksha | Samsara | Karma | Svarga
Which rishi's son Nachiketa learnt the secret of death from Yama? | Rishi Vajashravas | Rishi Vasistha | Rishi Bhrigu | Rishi Atri
The great illusion that hides the true reality is called what? | Maya | Lila | Prakriti | Karma
The divine play of God is called what? | Lila | Maya | Tapas | Yajna
Which mountain is described as the axis of the universe in the Puranas? | Mount Meru | Mount Kailash | Mount Abu | Mount Govardhan
The Ram Setu, the bridge to Lanka, was built by the vanaras under which architects? | Nala and Nila | Angada and Sugriva | Jambavan and Hanuman | Vibhishana and Angada
Which sacred river is said to flow unseen beneath the Triveni Sangam? | Saraswati | Sarayu | Sindhu | Gandaki
Which sage drank the entire ocean, according to the Puranas? | Rishi Agastya | Rishi Durvasa | Rishi Bhrigu | Rishi Atri
What is the name of the wish-fulfilling cow of the devas? | Kamadhenu | Airavata | Uchchaihshravas | Nandi
What is the name of the wish-fulfilling tree of the devas? | Kalpavriksha | Ashoka | Neem | Vata
What did the devas and asuras churn the ocean to obtain? | Amrita | Gold | Salt | Rain
Who drank the poison Halahala during the churning of the ocean? | Lord Shiva | Lord Vishnu | Indra Deva | Lord Brahma
